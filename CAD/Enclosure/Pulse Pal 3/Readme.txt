Manufacturing Notes

1. The Laser cut file is for 1.5mm acrylic. Lines under 0.05mm are to be vector cut, and anything larger is to be raster engraved or printed with UV ink.

2. Tolerances in the Pulse Pal 3 Body and Wing parts are optimized for production with Multijet Fusion / PA11.

3. Tolerances in the ScreenMount part are optimized for production by FDM with Prusa Core One / PETG.

4. Attach the laser-cut front panel to the 3D printed body using VHB tape on BOM.

5. Press square nuts (4) into square nut pockets on Pulse Pal 3 Body.

6. Use small sections of VHB Tape to seal square nuts into square nut pockets. They are a friction fit but may become loose on impact.

7. Silver thread-forming screws secure Pulse Pal board into enclosure (3) and laser-cut panel to rear of enclosure (4).

8. Black machine screws (2) secure wing to body.

9. Press light pipes into light pipe sockets on Pulse Pal 3 Body. The final section of the light pipe should press into place with significant resistance. Hammer gently with a rubber mallet if they cannot be pressed by hand (ensuring that all electronics and acrylic panels are removed from the body while hammering).
